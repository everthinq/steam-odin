# Team Fortress 2 — new case drops

When Valve adds a new case to Team Fortress 2, it drops while people play for
the first days and sells high before supply catches up. This page watches the
Team Fortress 2 news, starts ArchiSteamFarm playing Team Fortress 2 on your
accounts the minute a case is added, and lists the drops on the Steam Market.

Open it from the Dashboard tile **Team Fortress 2** (also /huginn/team-fortress).
Needs ASF ([getting-started.md](getting-started.md#5-card-farming-optional-for-andvari)).

> **Only accounts that can trade can sell.** Items dropped on a free-to-play
> account are not tradable or marketable. An account needs Team Fortress 2
> Premium for its drops to be sold; the accounts grid warns "not tradable".

## Buttons (they act at once, no second question)

| Button | Does |
|--------|------|
| **Play Team Fortress 2 now** / **Stop playing** | Start or stop the chosen accounts playing. Drops are limited per week, so start only when a new case is out |
| **Check news** | Read the Team Fortress 2 news now |
| **Sell now** | Read the inventories and list every item on the sell list now, for real |

## What runs by itself

- **Release watcher** (every 2 minutes): an update that adds a case → start
  playing, put the case on the sell list, send a Telegram message and ring
  your phone (each can be switched off).
- **Auto-sell**: reads each playing account's inventory every few minutes and
  lists sell-list items one minor unit under the lowest Market listing, then
  confirms only those listings.
- **Auto-stop**: stops playing after 24 hours (configurable).
- Every account gets the free Team Fortress 2 license automatically.

## Settings

Accounts to play (none ticked = all), poll minutes, auto-stop hours, Telegram
chat, and the **sell list** (one Market item name per line). Press **Save
settings**.
